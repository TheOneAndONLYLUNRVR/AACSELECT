PRIVACY POLICY

We collect as little as possible. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and we never sell data.

  • Nominations: the creator name and public handle/link you type. This is information about a third party; any nominee can ask us to remove it (see Takedown).
  • Ballots & nods: a random anonymous ID stored in your browser plus a one-way hashed IP address, used only to limit one ballot per person. Votes are not tied to your identity, and counts stay hidden until the reveal.
  • Chat (The Frequency): the display name and message you send, with a timestamp. It is public to every visitor; only the last 100 messages are kept.
  • Technical data: your IP address is processed briefly by the server and hosting provider for security, rate-limiting and the live “online” counter.
  • Retention: nominations and chat remain until removed, trimmed or reset. Ballot data is cleared at the end of each 7-day cycle.
  • Your rights: you can ask for access to or deletion of your data, or object to its processing, by contacting us. Please do not put sensitive personal information in chat or nominations.
  • Children: this site is not directed to children under 13.

COOKIES & BROWSER STORAGE

This site sets no cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage for essential features only: an anonymous voter ID (one ballot per person), which creators you have nodded, your ballot receipt, your chat name, sound on/off, which announcement you have already seen, and your acceptance of this notice. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings; doing so may reset your local receipts and preferences.
